INTERNSHIP & CAMPUS HIRING PLATFORM

1. Title

Internship & Campus Hiring Platform

2. Domain

Education Technology (EdTech) / Campus Recruitment Management System

3. Who is the User?

* Student
Creates profile and uploads resume.
Applies for internships and campus placements.
Tracks application status.

* Company Recruiter
Posts internship and job opportunities.
Reviews student applications.
Shortlists or rejects candidates.

* Admin
Manages students, recruiters, job postings, and platform activities.
Verifies recruiter accounts and monitors recruitment processes.


4. What Problem Are We Solving?

* Many colleges still manage internship and campus placement information through emails, spreadsheets, or notice boards, making the recruitment process slow and difficult to track. Students often miss opportunities due to delayed updates, while recruiters spend extra time handling applications manually. Colleges also face challenges in monitoring recruitment activities and maintaining placement records.

For example, if a company announces an internship through email, some students may miss it or apply after the deadline. This platform provides a centralized system where students can easily find opportunities, apply online, and track their application status in one place.

5. Proposed Solution

The application will provide a centralized platform for campus hiring and internship management.

Features:
Student registration and profile management.
Company recruiter registration and verification.
Internship and job posting management.
Online application submission.
Resume upload and viewing.
Application status tracking (Applied, Shortlisted, Selected, Rejected).
Search and filter job opportunities.
Admin dashboard for managing users and recruitment activities.
Notifications for new job postings and application updates.
Reports on placement statistics and recruitment activities.

8. Success Criteria
A student should be able to register and complete their profile in under 5 minutes.
Students should be able to apply for an internship or job in under 1 minute.
Recruiters should be able to post a new job within 2 minutes.
Application status should be updated accurately and visible to students.
Admin should be able to generate recruitment reports quickly and efficiently.

9. Out of Scope

The following features are not included in this project:

Online coding test platform.
Video interview integration.
AI-based resume screening.
Payment gateway integration.
College attendance management.
Payroll or employee management after hiring.
Mobile application development.
Third-party job portal integration.
